# Two former BioSFGroup members on Podcast Sekstant

[Former BioSFGroup members](../../group.md) [Vedran](https://vedran.miletic.net/) [Miletić](https://www.miletic.net/) and [Patrik](https://www.linkedin.com/in/patriknikolic) [Nikolić](https://nikoli.ch/) were featured in [Podcast Sekstant](https://www.youtube.com/c/podcastsekstant) episode 76, hosted by [Ilija Aščić](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ilija-a%C5%A1%C4%8Di%C4%87-3ba56347) and produced by [Filmić](https://filmic.hr/). The episode was recorded in room O-336 at the University of Rijeka, formerly [Željko Svedružić](../../principal-investigator.md)'s laboratory.

They discuss how artificial intelligence is changing opportunities for people starting their careers, which jobs may disappear, and how the technology can concentrate power and capital. They also explore investment in data centres, transhumanism, the migration of educated Europeans, and the state of Croatian scientific institutions and academia.
